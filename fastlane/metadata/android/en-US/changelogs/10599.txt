Dual SIM: with no default SIM the call screen asks which one to use instead of getting stuck, and every screen follows your SIM choice. Keypad: long press 0 gives + again, * and # add a pause and a wait (with "send tones" during the call), long press 1 calls voicemail, secret codes work, the missed-call notification opens history. Calls: decline with a message, and end-and-answer for a second call.
